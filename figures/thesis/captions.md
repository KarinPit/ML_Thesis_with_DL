## F01_study_area

Study domain (30.25–43.00°N, 10.00–38.75°E, 0.25° grid) and mean WWLLN lightning stroke density for October–December of the training years (2013–2020), in strokes km⁻² yr⁻¹. The orange box marks the area used for the comparison with the Israeli network (ILDN).

## F02_pipeline

Data and model pipeline. 8 input fields averaged over 12-h windows enter a U-Net-style encoder–decoder that outputs the expected number of strokes per cell (a Poisson rate λ). The network is trained with a Poisson loss against WWLLN strokes; its output is then calibrated by quantile mapping fitted on the validation year.

## F03_ablation

Ablation from the set-up of Jones et al. (2026) to the final model. Each row adds one change; points show the mean FSS (3 × 3 cells, ≥ 1 stroke per window) on the test years with 95 % block-bootstrap confidence intervals. Total gain: 0.16. The dashed line is the calibrated CAPE × P baseline.

## F04_fss_vs_scale

(a) Fractions Skill Score for ≥ 1 stroke per 12 h as a function of neighbourhood size, test years 2022, 2024. Shading: 95 % block-bootstrap confidence intervals. Dotted line: useful-skill level 0.5 + f₀/2 (f₀ = 0.044). At 3 × 3 cells the U-Net reaches 0.61 [0.54, 0.67] against 0.53 [0.47, 0.58] for calibrated CAPE × P. (b) FSS at 3 × 3 cells by month.

## F05_bias_maps

(a) Observed mean WWLLN stroke density on the test years (strokes km⁻² yr⁻¹, Oct–Dec rate). (b) U-Net minus observed and (c) CAPE × P (constant fitted on the training years) minus observed. CAPE × P: mean bias -0.266, significant in 16 % of cells.

## F07_skill_by_intensity

## F08_reliability

(a) Reliability diagram for the probability of at least one stroke per cell and window. For the U-Net and CAPE × P it is 1 − e^(−λ) with λ the predicted Poisson rate; for climatology it is the training-year frequency per cell and month. BSS: Brier skill score relative to climatology. Inset: number of U-Net forecasts per probability bin (log scale). (b) Performance diagram: probability of detection against success ratio for probability thresholds 0.02–0.98; grey curves are CSI, dashed lines frequency bias. U-Net: BSS -0.40, ROC AUC 0.97, PR AUC 0.65.
